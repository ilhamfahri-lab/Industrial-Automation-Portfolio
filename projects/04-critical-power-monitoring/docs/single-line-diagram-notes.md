# Conceptual Single-Line Diagram Notes

This project may include a conceptual monitoring diagram of utility supply, generator, transfer equipment, UPS, and monitored loads. It is not an electrical design for construction.

Required diagram labels:
- CONCEPTUAL — SIMULATION ONLY.
- Distinguish monitoring/data points from power paths.
- Mark synthetic values and assumed states.
- Do not imply validation of short-circuit calculations, protection settings, grounding, arc-flash analysis, redundancy, or equipment sizing.
- Use no real facility or confidential diagram details.
