# Project 05 — Industrial OT Network & Troubleshooting Lab

> Status: Planned — isolated lab design and tests have not been completed.

## Overview
A defensive lab design for PLC/HMI/SCADA communications, addressing, zones, approved traffic analysis, and repeatable troubleshooting.

**Target roles:** OT Engineer; Control Systems Engineer; Industrial Network Support Engineer.

## Planned toolchain
Cisco Packet Tracer, Wireshark, draw.io, KEPServerEX where useful, Git.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Lab zones, device roles, and addressing are documented.
- [ ] Allowed communication paths have a business justification.
- [ ] At least three controlled troubleshooting cases are recorded.
- [ ] Captures come only from an owned or explicitly authorized lab.

This is a defensive lab; do not probe production OT networks without explicit authorization.
